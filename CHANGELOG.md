# Changelog

## 0.1.0

Initial release of MultilevelKernelInterpolation.jl.

- Multilevel kernel interpolation for fast matrix-vector products.
- Equidistant and Chebyshev Lagrange bases, and buffered, stabilized B-splines.
- Dense and FFT-based Toeplitz couplings, with supported symmetry reductions.
- Galerkin and Petrov-Galerkin maps with BEAST moment assembly.
- Shared cores, separate workspaces, and threaded execution through H2Factory.
